The Warm Up!

Before you get really going, it’s best to warm up your body to increase your circulation and reduce the chance of injury! Light cardio can work wonders! Aim for 5 minutes if you can!

Walk: Take a brisk walk around the room or just walk in place for at least 2 minutes!

Arm Circles: Stick your arms out and rotate them 10 times going forwards, and 10 times going backwards.

Torso Twists: Stand up straight and twist your torso from left to right. Do 10 times to each side. Aim for at least 30 seconds of torso twists.

Squats: Try to squat your own body weight. Just bend your knees and squat. Do as many as you can for 1 minute!

Leg and Glute Stretches/ Lunges: Stand up straight with both feet together. Then take the biggest step forward as far as you can without falling. And return to your starting position. You've just done a lunge! Now, try to do 5 lunges on each leg!

If you do more, that’s great! Light or brisk walking can help with blood flow. Mixing brisk walking with periodic speed-ups challenges the heart and improves endurance. This is the walking routine that helped me get started! Week 1 (60 minutes)- Make it a habit! The first week is all about forming a routine and making walking for exercise feel natural. Don’t focus on distance or speed. Just get out there! Pick a time and speed that allows you to walk for 20 to 30 minutes. Try to fit in at least 3 of those walks during the first week. Week 2 (60 to 80 minutes) - Go longer! By the end of week 1, you’ve worked out for at least 1 hour. That’s a good start! Now, in week 2, try to walk a little longer while still staying comfortable. Mild muscle fatigue is normal. You are working towards these walks becoming routine. Your body will get used to it. Week 3 (60 to 80 minutes) – Switch it up! During week 3, try a different walking route. Get a change of scenery. The new route helps change the course of how your walks go by adding new terrain or resistance. If using a treadmill, increase the incline to 1 or 2%. This helps you to prepare your body to endure whatever obstacles may come before you. Week 4 (60 to 90 minutes) – Build your endurance! You’ve made it to week 4! I knew you could do it! This is when you start trying out new things. Try to conquer a hill or two on your route. If using a treadmill, increase the incline a bit more. You’ll be surprised by the difference between 2 and 5%. Try to go for at least 10 minutes longer than you did on your best day last week. As you keep progressing, you’ll start to realize that you can do more than what you thought that you could. Week 5 and beyond (90+ minutes... but at least 60 minutes) – You've been on this journey for about a month now. I hope you are feeling stronger! I knew that you had it in you! Don’t stop now! We’ve been walking this whole time. Now, let’s sprinkle in some running or light jogging here and there. Follow the same technique to train yourself to run. Start small and work your way up. Remember, you’ve got this!
